SoliC — Solar Panel Self-Cleaning System

Conrad Challenge Innovator · 2024–2025

A passive solar-panel cleaning system combining a dew collector with super-hydrophobic and super-hydrophilic panel coatings.

Outcome

Why

Dust on panels quietly destroys solar yield, and washing them costs water and labor — especially wrong in the dry, dusty places solar works best.

My role

Product designer and video maker. Invented and developed the dew collector, the system's major innovation; contributed to adopting the hydrophobic/hydrophilic coating approach.
