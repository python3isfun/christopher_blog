Evaluating Radicalization through Autism Diagnosis Criteria and Media Addiction

UCSB Summer Research Academies, Track 12 “Digital Brains” · Completed, Summer 2025
First author · Jun 2025 – Jul 2025

A 4-week study asking which specific autism diagnostic traits — not autism as a monolith — relate to radicalization susceptibility.

Outcome

Extremism correlated most with circumscribed interests; violent extremism correlated most with sensory/motor deficiencies. Problematic media use was not a significant moderating factor.

Why

Existing work either treats autistic populations as homogenous or relies on narrow journalistic case studies, leaving no usable foundation for prevention programs.

Method

Ritvo Autism Asperger Diagnostic Scale–Revised for autism symptomatology, the Problematic Media Use Measure for digital media addiction, and the Extremism Scale plus the Pro-violence and Illegal Acts in Relation to Extremism Scale for radicalization outcomes.

What it means

Results are meant as a foundation for informed, trait-specific radicalization prevention support systems rather than blanket assumptions.
