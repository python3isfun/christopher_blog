Measure Before You Reconstruct: Remote Measurement of Residual-Limb Circumference from Sparse Smartphone Silhouettes

Manuscript in preparation · Authoring, fall 2026
Sole author · independent research · Jan 2026 – present

A remote prosthetic-socket sizing pipeline that measures limb circumference from a handful of calibrated phone photos — no dense 3D reconstruction needed.

Outcome

Applying Cauchy's projection formula to calibrated smartphone silhouettes hit 97.5–99.8% spectral accuracy across 33 OpenLimbTT models (validated by Fourier analysis), while eliminating COLMAP's dense-reconstruction overhead. Benchmarked against VGGT to identify failure modes.

Why

e-NABLE volunteers fit recipients on the other side of the world from photos. Better remote sizing means better-fitting devices for the Uganda and West Bank cases our chapter works on.

Method

Cauchy-based silhouette measurement algorithm with perspective corrections; Fourier analysis for spectral validation against the OpenLimbTT model set.

What it means

Measuring directly from silhouettes beats reconstructing geometry first, for this task.
