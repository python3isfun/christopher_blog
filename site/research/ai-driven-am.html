Artificial Intelligence Driven Design and Geometry Optimization in Additive Manufacturing

National High School Journal of Science (NHSJS) · Passed peer review, July 2026
Sole author · Sept 2025 – Jul 2026

A structured literature review of 19 studies (2018–2026) on how AI actually supports additive-manufacturing design decisions.

Outcome

Thematic synthesis identified four connected roles for AI in AM design: accelerating design search, generating candidate geometries, coordinating decisions across structural scales, and folding manufacturing- and process-aware constraints into optimization.

Why

AM design and geometry optimization are bottlenecked by repeated simulation, very large design spaces, and manufacturing constraints that most optimizers ignore.

Method

Scopus as the primary database with Google Scholar for citation tracing; screened 2018–2026 publications down to 19 core studies; compared them across AI method, AM process, geometry type, validation approach, performance metrics, and reported limitations.

What it means

Key gaps remain: limited datasets, weak generalization testing, low interpretability, limited process adaptivity, and underdeveloped industrial qualification standards.
