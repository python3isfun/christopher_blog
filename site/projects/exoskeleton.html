Load-Bearing Upper Body Exoskeleton

Central California Invention Convention & Rotary 3D Printing Challenge finalist · 2026

A passive upper-body exoskeleton that supports safe manual handling and improves ergonomic performance in high-strain environments.

Outcome

Finalist in both the 2026 Central California Invention Convention and the 2026 Rotary Young Innovators 3D Printing Challenge.

Why

Repetitive lifting injuries are a preventable cost of manual work; a printable passive support puts load off the lower back without a powered system.

My role

Designer.
